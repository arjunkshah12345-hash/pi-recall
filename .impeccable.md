## Design Context

### Users
Hackathon judges, Supermemory reviewers, and AI hardware builders evaluating whether Pi Recall is real, reproducible, and meaningfully centered on Supermemory Local.

### Brand Personality
Precise, grounded, and memorable. The product should feel like a serious local-first hardware assistant, not a generic AI landing page.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a clean technical editorial style with warm local-hardware cues, dense but readable proof points, and a strong first-screen explanation of the memory loop. Avoid neon AI gradients, vague chatbot imagery, and oversized SaaS hero fluff.

### Design Principles
- Make Supermemory Local the main story in the first viewport.
- Show evidence: architecture, commands, checks, and repo links.
- Keep the page fast, static, and easy for judges to scan.
- Use restrained motion and high contrast.
- Prefer concrete product language over hype.
